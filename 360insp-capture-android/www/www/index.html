How this works / troubleshooting
Two ways to capture, per pin:
• SNAP 360° drives the camera directly over its Wi-Fi (OSC: setOptions → takePicture → download the stitched JPG) and auto-attaches it to the selected pin.
• ATTACH lets you pick a photo already on the phone (e.g. imported by the Insta360 app) and binds + renames it to the pin. This always works.

If SNAP fails: browsers block a secure (https) page from calling the camera's plain-http address, and may block cross-origin reads. For direct capture, load this file over http (e.g. a local server or the file opened directly) while on the camera Wi-Fi, or just use ATTACH. The log in the Camera panel shows the exact error.

Offline: open this page once on normal internet so it caches, then switch to the camera's Wi-Fi. Pins, photos and the PDF live in this browser and survive reloads.
